Lasagna
Ingredients:
  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • 500g ground beef or pork
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp garlic (minced)
  • 1 tsp dried oregano or mixed herbs
  • 1 cup cottage cheese or ricotta
  • 2 cups grated mozzarella
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Method:
  • Cook noodles as per package instructions.
  • Sauté onion and garlic in a pan. Add ground meat, cook until browned. Stir in tomato sauce and herbs.
  • Layer in a baking dish: meat sauce, noodles, cheese. Repeat and top with mozzarella.
  • Bake at 180°C (350°F) for 30–35 minutes.

Cauliflower Fried Rice
Ingredients:
  • 1 small head of cauliflower, grated
  • 3 tbsp reduced-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp mirin
  • 1 tsp granulated sugar
  • ¼ cup neutral oil
  • 2 shallots, thinly sliced
  • 2 large eggs, beaten
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • 3 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  • ½ cup frozen peas
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Method:
  • Heat oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
  • Sauté shallots, garlic, and carrot until tender.
  • Add grated cauliflower and cook for 5 minutes.
  • Push mixture to the side, scramble eggs in the pan, then mix with vegetables.
  • Stir in peas, soy sauce, mirin, and sugar. Cook until heated through.
Cinnamon Roll Pancakes
Ingredients:
  • 1¾ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 2 cups buttermilk
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • Cinnamon Filling: 4 tbsp melted butter, ⅓ cup brown sugar, 1½ tsp cinnamon
  • Glaze: 4 tbsp butter, 2 oz cream cheese, ¾ cup powdered sugar, ½ tsp vanilla
Method:
  • In a bowl, mix flour, baking powder, and salt. In another bowl, whisk buttermilk, eggs, vanilla, and oil. Combine wet and dry ingredients.
  • Prepare the cinnamon filling by mixing melted butter, brown sugar, and cinnamon. Place in a piping bag.
  • For the glaze, beat butter and cream cheese until smooth. Add powdered sugar and vanilla; mix until creamy.
  • Heat a skillet over medium heat and grease it. Pour ¼ cup batter for each pancake. Once bubbles form, pipe a spiral of cinnamon filling on top. Flip and cook until golden.
  • Serve pancakes drizzled with cream cheese glaze.
    • Crepes
    Ingredients:
    • 1½ cups whole or 2% milk
    • 2 large eggs
    • 1 cup all-purpose flour
    • Pinch of salt
    • 1 tbsp granulated sugar
    • 3 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
    Method:
  • In a blender, combine milk, eggs, flour, salt, sugar, and melted butter. Blend until smooth.
  • Let the batter rest for 30 minutes.
  • Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat and lightly grease it.
  • Pour ¼ cup of batter into the skillet, swirling to coat the bottom.
  • Cook for 1-2 minutes until the edges lift, then flip and cook for another minute.
  • Repeat with remaining batter. Serve with desired fillings.

    Ground Pork Stir-Fry
    Ingredients:
    • 500g ground pork
    • 1 small onion, chopped
    • 1 bell pepper, sliced
    • 2 tbsp soy sauce
    • 1 tbsp oil (for frying)
    • 1 tsp garlic, minced
    • 1 tsp ginger, grated (optional)
    • Salt and pepper to taste
    • Cooked rice for serving
    Method:
    • Heat oil in a pan. Add onion and garlic, sauté until soft.
    • Add ground pork, cook until browned.
    • Add bell pepper, soy sauce, and seasoning. Stir fry for 5–7 minutes.
    • Serve over hot rice.
    Brownies
    Ingredients:
    • ½ cup (1 stick) butter, melted
    • 1 cup sugar
    • 2 eggs
    • ½ cup flour
    • ⅓ cup cocoa powder
    • ½ tsp baking powder
    • ½ tsp salt
    • 1 tsp vanilla extract
    Method:
    • Preheat oven to 175°C (350°F). Grease an 8x8 inch pan.
    • Mix melted butter and sugar. Add eggs and vanilla.
    • Sift in dry ingredients and mix just until combined.
    • Pour into pan and bake for 20–25 mins.
    Pasta Alfredo
    Ingredients:
    • 200g pasta (fettuccine or penne)
    • 1 cup heavy cream
    • 2 tbsp butter
    • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
    • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
    • Salt & pepper to taste
    • Parsley (for garnish)
    Method:
    • Cook pasta according to package instructions.
    • In a pan, melt butter and sauté garlic.
    • Add cream and simmer. Stir in cheese, salt, and pepper until smooth.
    • Mix in the pasta and top with parsley.
    Fruit Parfait
    Ingredients:
    • 1 cup yogurt (plain or flavored)
    • 1/2 cup granola
    • 1/2 cup mixed fruits (berries, banana, mango, etc.)
    • 1 tsp honey (optional)
    Method:
    • In a glass or bowl, layer yogurt, granola, and fruit.

    • Drizzle with honey on top.
    • Chill before serving.
    Chicken Salad
    Ingredients:
    • 2 cups cooked chicken (shredded or chopped)
    • ½ cup mayonnaise or plain yogurt
    • ¼ cup chopped celery or cucumber
    • ¼ cup chopped onion (optional)
    • 1 tsp mustard (optional)
    • Salt and pepper to taste
    • Lettuce or bread for serving
    Method:
    • Mix chicken, mayo/yogurt, and veggies in a bowl.
    • Season with salt, pepper, and mustard if using.
    • Serve chilled over lettuce or in a sandwich.
